About VIT Vellore

Founded in 1984 by visionary educator Dr. G. Viswanathan, Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) has evolved into one of India's most prestigious institutions, recognized globally for innovation, research, and holistic education.

🏛️

Institution of Eminence

Recognized as an Institution of Eminence (IoE) by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, empowering VIT to deliver world-class infrastructure and global academic standards.

  • NAAC 4th Cycle Accredited with A++ Grade
  • Global ABET (USA) Engineering Accreditations
  • Over 372-acre green smart campus in Katpadi, Vellore
📚

Fully Flexible Credit System

Pioneering education with FFCS, enabling students to curate their individual timetables, choose preferred faculty, and balance fast-track courses with industry internships.

  • Choose Your Course & Preferred Faculty Slot
  • 9-Pointer Privilege (Attendance Exemption for CGPA ≥ 9.0)
  • Cross-disciplinary Minors and Honors Degrees
💼

Prime Career Placements

Renowned Career Development Centre (CDC) with consistent national records, attracting world-renowned tech giants and top multinational product leaders.

  • Dream (₹5.5L+) & Super Dream (₹10L+) Tiers
  • Marquee Offers up to ₹1.02 Crore
  • Limca Book of Records for campus recruitment

B.Tech Admissions & VITEEE

Admission to B.Tech programmes is conducted strictly through the computer-based VITEEE rank merit list.

  • 10+2 with 60% aggregate in PCM/PCB (50% for SC/ST & J&K/NE)
  • 125 Multiple Choice Questions (No negative marking)
  • 100% Transparent Online Counseling in rank phases

Tuition Fees & GVSDP Scholarships

Tuition fees are structured transparently across 5 categories based on VITEEE rank merit.

  • Category 1 Group A: Starts at ~₹1,73,000/yr
  • Category 1 Group B (CSE, ECE, Mech): ₹1,98,000/yr
  • GVSDP 100% waiver for State and Central Board toppers

Popular Frequently Asked Questions

Verified answers to the most common queries asked by students and parents.

For Group B branches (including Computer Science and Engineering, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, and IT), Category 1 tuition starts at ₹1,98,000 per year. For Group A branches (Biotechnology, Civil, Chemical), Category 1 tuition starts at ~₹1,73,000 per year, plus a one-time refundable caution deposit of ₹3,000.

Source: VIT B.Tech Fee Structure

Students maintaining a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 9.00 or above ('9-pointers') are exempt from the mandatory 75% minimum attendance requirement. However, they must still complete all internal assessments, quizzes, labs, CAT exams, and the FAT.

Source: VIT Academic Regulations

Yes. Candidates with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) in 10+2 are eligible for B.Tech programmes in Biotechnology, Biomedical Engineering, and CSE with specialization in Bioinformatics. They take the Biology section in VITEEE instead of Mathematics.

Source: VITEEE Eligibility Criteria

Hostel biometric attendance is taken every evening. For first-year students, curfew is generally 8:30 PM to 9:00 PM; for senior students, curfew is 9:00 PM. Outings and leaves are managed digitally through the VTOP e-gate pass system with digital warden and parent verification.

Source: VIT Hostel Code of Conduct

The Career Development Centre (CDC) categorizes placement offers by compensation: Regular Offers (up to ₹5.5 LPA), Dream Offers (between ₹5.5 LPA and ₹10 LPA), Super Dream Offers (above ₹10 LPA), and Marquee Offers (premier product companies offering ₹25 LPA to ₹1 Crore+).

Source: VIT Placements Overview
